# ADR 0007: Remediation Safety/Approval Gates

**Status**
Accepted

**Context**
Automated actions can amplify incidents.

**Decision**
Policies consider risk, severity, and explicit approval and return decisions without side effects.

**Alternatives**
Unreviewed destructive automation.

**Consequences**
Safety is enforceable at policy boundary.

**Explicit Non-goals**
No execution, credentials, or change-management replacement.
